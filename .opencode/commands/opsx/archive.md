---
description: Synchronize and archive a completed OpenSpec-compatible change
---

Use the knowledge-maintenance skill. Archive completed change `$1` only after checking
that its tasks are complete, verification is recorded, and its approved spec deltas are
synchronized into `openspec/specs/`. Move the change to
`openspec/changes/archive/YYYY-MM-DD-$1/`, update `docs/state/current.md`, and retain
links to any unresolved follow-up work. Do not archive incomplete or unverified work.
